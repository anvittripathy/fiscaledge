Anvit Tripathy handles every matter personally — from initial review through reply drafting, departmental hearings, and appellate representation. I received a GST notice. How urgent is this? +
You have 30 days to respond. Most businesses lose the case at this stage — not at adjudication or appeal. Your first reply creates the legal record for every subsequent proceeding. Strategic errors here are almost never reversible. Submit your notice for a personal review within 24 hours — fiscaledgeindia@gmail.com or +91 80006 59645.
Can blocked ITC be recovered after a GSTR-2B mismatch? +
Yes. ITC blocked due to supplier non-filing, GSTR-2B mismatches, or classification issues can often be recovered through full reconciliation, rectified returns, and structured departmental engagement. FiscalEdge has recovered ₹200 Cr+ across client engagements. Submit for a free assessment.
What is FiscalEdge's fee structure? +
Fees are matter-specific, discussed after reviewing your situation. We do not quote without understanding the matter. Initial case assessment is without charge. Engagement fee is proposed clearly after the first session — specific to your matter, no hidden charges. Not all matters are accepted.
Are pre-GST Service Tax demands still a risk? +
Yes — pre-GST demands are actively litigated and accumulate 18% interest p.a. The longer they remain open, the more they compound. FiscalEdge specializes in clean, documented closure of legacy matters under Central Excise, Service Tax, and VAT — including matters open 5–10+ years.

Yes — pan-India practice. Headquartered in Surat, Anvit Tripathy handles matters for clients in Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and across India. Departmental proceedings and appellate matters are handled in the relevant jurisdictions.
